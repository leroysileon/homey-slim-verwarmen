Smart Heating gives your thermostat a weekly schedule that learns, without a subscription.

Built for the tado X connected to Homey via Matter, it works with any thermostat in Homey that reports a target and a measured temperature.

Weekly schedule
Set time blocks per day on the app's settings page. Copy a day to all weekdays, the weekend or the whole week.

Manual changes always win
Turn the knob on the wall, change the temperature in another app or in Homey: that setting stays until the next moment in the schedule, after which the schedule takes over again.

Warm on time
Every time the heating has to warm up, the app measures how long your boiler needs to get going and how many degrees per hour your home warms up, taking the outdoor temperature into account. It then starts early enough to reach the set temperature on time.

Home and away
Use the Flow cards to switch to an eco temperature when everyone has left and back to the schedule when someone comes home.

Widget and Climate
Add the Heating widget to your Home screen to see the temperature, nudge it up or down and see today's schedule at a glance. Add the Smart Heating device to see the schedule in Homey's Climate overview.

Note: the settings page is currently in Dutch.
